CampusFlow 

A simple and aesthetic student productivity dashboard designed to help students organise their academic life in one place.

> Features

•⁠  ⁠Dashboard: View tasks, completed work, pending tasks, and study progress.
•⁠  ⁠Task Manager: Add, complete, and delete tasks.
•⁠  ⁠Timetable: Organise your classes by day and time.
•⁠  ⁠Study Planner: Schedule study sessions and track your learning plans.
•⁠  ⁠My Notes: Create and manage personal study notes.
•⁠  ⁠Local Storage: Keep your data saved in your browser.
•⁠  ⁠Responsive Design: Use the dashboard on different screen sizes.

> Technologies Used

•⁠  ⁠HTML5
•⁠  ⁠CSS3
•⁠  ⁠JavaScript
•⁠  ⁠Browser Local Storage

> How to Run

1.⁠ ⁠Download or clone this repository.
2.⁠ ⁠Open the project folder.
3.⁠ ⁠Open index.html in your browser.
4.⁠ ⁠Start organising your academic tasks!

> Data Storage

CampusFlow uses browser Local Storage. Your tasks, timetable, study sessions, and notes are saved in the browser you use. They are not synchronised across devices.
